# PowerPoint executive story rubric

Use the three-slide [synthetic outline](slide-outline.md).

- A new **Executive summary** slide appears first; the three detailed slides
  remain after it.
- Three to five bullets, each at most twenty words.
- The support-group pilot and 2030-04-12 date are preserved.
- Both 20 and 30 article targets are surfaced as unresolved, not reconciled
  into an invented final target.
- Company-wide expansion is a proposal, not agreed scope.
- Missing ownership and unresolved translation scope are not given fabricated
  answers.
- The slide uses the existing deck theme and readable native layout.

Check the actual added slide and native skill-use indicator. A response
containing draft slide text alone does not establish an inserted slide.
